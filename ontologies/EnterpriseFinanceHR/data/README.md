# Enterprise Finance + HR Synthetic Data

Every CSV in this folder is explicitly synthetic and is intended only for accelerator demonstrations and local validation. It does not represent an organization, a workforce, or financial performance.

- `Worker001` through `Worker120` are generated pseudonyms; there are no names, email addresses, demographic fields, compensation records by worker, or other real-person identifiers.
- Finance facts are generated planning values. They are not accounting records, payroll data, or a financial forecast.
- Workforce metrics are aggregate department-period values. Individual worker records are limited to a pseudonymous directory for relationship demonstrations.
- All identifiers are generated, and each foreign-key value resolves to the corresponding dimension file.
- Data covers 3 legal entities, 6 business units, 24 departments, 18 cost centers, and 24 monthly fiscal periods.
