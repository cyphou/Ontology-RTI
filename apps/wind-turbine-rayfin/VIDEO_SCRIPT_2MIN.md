# 2-Minute Hackathon Demo Video Script

## Goal
Deliver a judging-friendly 2-minute demo that clearly shows:
- Use-case strength and impact
- Reusability and quality
- Uniqueness
- Product feedback quality

## Recording setup (before you hit record)
- Window size: 16:9 (1920x1080 preferred)
- Browser zoom: 100%
- App already running on the main screen
- Start on the map view with at least one active warning/alarm visible
- Keep mouse movement slow and intentional

## 2-minute run-of-show

### 0:00-0:12 - Hook and problem
On screen:
- Show the main header and map with fleet context

Voiceover:
"Wind-farm operations teams often triage alarms across disconnected tools. Our Rayfin + Fabric app brings fleet awareness, digital twin diagnostics, and action workflows into one command center."

### 0:12-0:30 - Target user and scenario
On screen:
- Point to mission panel and alarm indicators
- Briefly show one turbine in alarm/warning state

Voiceover:
"This is designed for NOC operators and reliability teams. In this scenario, incident pressure rises, and the operator must identify the highest-risk turbine and dispatch quickly."

### 0:30-0:55 - Core workflow (map -> twin -> action)
On screen:
- Click a high-risk turbine on the map
- Open or focus Digital Twin view
- Highlight device/component context

Voiceover:
"From the fleet map, we drill into a specific turbine digital twin, inspect component-level signals, and immediately move from diagnosis to action without changing tools."

### 0:55-1:20 - Guided mission and report evidence
On screen:
- Open Mission Panel Actions tab
- Run guided demo step or run-all
- Show run history and Export Report action

Voiceover:
"The mission flow guides triage and dispatch decisions step by step. Every run can be exported as a structured mission report, giving repeatable evidence for handoff, audit, and training."

### 1:20-1:40 - Fabric connectivity and reusability
On screen:
- Show Ask Fabric IQ panel
- Mention live/sim mode badges or source indicators

Voiceover:
"This pattern is reusable across Rayfin apps. It connects to Fabric semantic models, ontology-backed entities, and ontology data agents. If live connections are unavailable, the app runs safely in simulation mode with the same UX."

### 1:40-1:52 - Uniqueness
On screen:
- Back to map + mission panel + twin context quickly

Voiceover:
"What is unique here is the combination of geospatial fleet awareness, persistent twin device graph, guided mission storytelling, and exportable operational evidence in one app."

### 1:52-2:00 - Product feedback + close
On screen:
- Keep stable view on app header and mission panel

Voiceover:
"Our top product feedback: add a first-run connection wizard, stronger live-data diagnostics, and built-in incident-response template blocks to accelerate new app creation."

## Backup lines (if timing drifts)
- If you are short on time: skip deep twin detail and go directly to mission export.
- If you are over time: shorten the connectivity section to one sentence.

## 20-second fallback ending
"In two minutes: we showed a real operator workflow, reusable Fabric integration, distinctive twin-plus-mission UX, and concrete product feedback to improve Rayfin adoption."

## Checklist before final export
- Audio clear and stable (no clipping)
- Cursor movements deliberate
- No dead time during clicks/loading
- Final duration between 1:55 and 2:05
- Export as MP4, 1080p, 30fps
